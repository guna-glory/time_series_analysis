A Time Series Analysis and Forecasting project that analyzes **energy demand data** and applies the **ARIMA (AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average)** model for forecasting.

The dataset contains information about:

- Electricity demand
- Solar energy generation
- Timestamp of each observation

The main goal is to understand the behavior of energy demand over time and use **ARIMA** to forecast future demand.

🤖 ARIMA

The main forecasting technique used in this project is ARIMA.

ARIMA stands for:

ARIMA
│
├── AR → AutoRegressive
├── I  → Integrated
└── MA → Moving Average

It is commonly used for forecasting time-series data by modeling relationships within historical observations.

AR — AutoRegressive

Uses previous values of the time series to predict future values.

I — Integrated

Uses differencing to make the time series more suitable for modeling when required.

MA — Moving Average

Uses previous forecasting errors to improve future predictions.

Together, these components form the ARIMA model used for time-series forecasting.
